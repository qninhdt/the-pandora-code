<Sentence/Component 28>
- **EN:** <WhatThisMeans>
The signature of a living planet is not a goddess and not a global mind. It is the active, ceaseless maintenance of a thermodynamic *disequilibrium* — an atmosphere, a chemistry, a climate held far from where dead physics would leave them, paid for by a constant throughput of energy from a star. By that measure Earth and Pandora are both unmistakably alive. The difference is only in the wiring. Earth regulates itself the way a watershed does — emergently, blindly, with the regulation falling out of physics for free. Pandora regulates itself the way a body does — through an integrated network that senses and responds as one. Earth is the real science; Pandora is the thought experiment that lets you *see* the difference, by building the version Earth could have been and isn't.
</WhatThisMeans>
