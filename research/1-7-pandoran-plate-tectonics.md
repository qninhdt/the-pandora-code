## Part A - Geoscientific Inventory of Pandoran Canon

### 1. Mountain Ranges, Escarpments, and Highland Geomorphology

Pandora’s observable surface displays high vertical relief, steep structural escarpments, isolated karst-like monoliths, and extensive upland plateaus. Canonical materials establish several distinct geomorphic provinces:

* **The Hallelujah Mountains (*Ayram Alusìng*):** Massive monolithic bedrock masses, measuring from hundreds of meters to several kilometers in diameter, suspended in sub-planetary levitation within regions of extreme magnetic flux density (*Flux Vortex*). Official production design and concept artwork explicitly model their morphological appearance on subaerially weathered tower karst and sandstone pillar landscapes (specifically the quartz-sandstone pillars of Wulingyuan in Zhangjiajie, China). However, their suspension mechanism is canonically attributed to the Meissner effect and magnetic flux pinning operating on high-concentration deposits of superconducting unobtanium embedded within the rock mass. The floating mountains themselves do not represent an orogenic (collisional) thrust system; rather, they are detached blocks of the upper lithosphere lifted and locked into magnetic gradients. The non-floating highlands beneath and surrounding the Hallelujah range feature deeply incised valleys, vertical cliff faces, and high-standing plateaus. Official art assets depict these base highlands as dissected massifs, but canonical texts provide no collisional, compressive, or folding mechanism for their primary tectonic uplift.
* **The Stone Arches (*Malmok* or *Ayrang*):** Colossal arched rock formations enclosing the basin that hosts the Tree of Souls (*Vitraya Ramunong*). The largest individual arches reach spans of $500\text{ m}$ and rise $300\text{ m}$ above the floor. Canonical lore provides an explicit petrogenetic formation mechanism: these structures formed during Pandora's primordial molten epoch, when intense, localized magnetic field loops distorted iron-rich, unobtanium-bearing silicate melts into arcuate geometries prior to crystallization. Subsequent subaerial weathering and mechanical erosion stripped away the less resistant surrounding country rock over geological time, exhuming the resistant arch skeletons.
* **The Ashlands Province:** A regional volcanic terrain inhabited by the Mangkwan clan, characterized by basaltic lava plains, pyroclastic sheets, cinder cones, and major volcanic calderas. The catastrophic eruption that destroyed the ancestral Mangkwan settlement produced widespread ash fall and ignimbrite sheets, indicating volatile-rich explosive volcanism rather than passive effusive venting alone.
* **Mons Veritatis and High Camp Escarpments:** Vertically sheer cliff networks and stepped plateau rims exhibiting over $1,000\text{ m}$ of exposed structural relief. Official tie-in materials classify these as erosional and fault-bounded plateaus, though whether the bounding structures are normal faults, reverse faults, or strike-slip boundaries is nowhere canonically defined.

### 2. Ocean Basins, Coastlines, and Global Hypsometry

Pandora’s surface supports a global marine hydrosphere featuring deep open oceans, shallow inland seas, and complex archipelagos inhabited by the Metkayina and other reef clans:

* **Reef Systems and Archipelagos:** The eastern marine territories feature extensive atoll-like ring structures, shallow carbonate platforms, and emergent islets such as the Three Brothers Rocks and the Cove of the Ancestors. Visual and ecological worldbuilding portrays these islands as biogenic carbonate complexes growing atop subsiding volcanic foundations, mimicking terrestrial Darwinian atoll subsidence sequences. The Three Brothers formations consist of steeply inclined, columnar-jointed basaltic sea stacks, indicating localized volcanic extrusion followed by marine wave erosion.
* **Global Hypsometry and Crustal Division:** Terrestrial elevation exhibits a strictly bimodal distribution reflecting two petrologically distinct crustal types: low-density felsic continental crust ($\text{mean elevation } +840\text{ m}$) and dense mafic oceanic crust ($\text{mean depth } -3,700\text{ m}$). Orbital views and cartographic projections of Pandora depict large continental landmasses separated by deep oceanic realms. However, canonical sources contain no bathymetric soundings, seismic Moho profiles, or formal hypsometric curves. It remains canonically unverified whether Pandoran ocean basins represent thin, dense basaltic oceanic crust generated at mid-ocean spreading ridges, or depressions formed within a single, continuous, compositionally uniform crust (unimodal hypsometry, analogous to Mars or Venus).

### 3. Spatial Distribution and Petrological Style of Volcanism

Volcanism is confirmed across Pandora, but its planetary-scale spatial distribution remains largely unmapped:

* **Eruption Dynamics in the Ashlands:** The devastation of the Mangkwan clan’s homeland was driven by explosive, volatile-rich volcanism (pyroclastic flows, sub-plinian ash plumes, and extensive tephra blanketing). This behavior requires intermediate-to-felsic magmas with substantial volatile budgets ($\mathrm{H_2O}$, $\mathrm{CO_2}$, $\mathrm{SO_2}$), distinct from the dry, effusive ultramafic silicate volcanism observed on Io.
* **Spatial Distribution Patterns:** On Earth, volcanism is organized along narrow linear plate boundaries (subduction-zone volcanic arcs and mid-ocean ridge spreading axes) with isolated intraplate hotspot chains. Conversely, Io and Mars display broadly distributed paterae and stationary volcanic shields. Official Pandoran sources provide no global volcanic maps; no linear volcanic arcs, back-arc basins, or age-progressive hotspot tracks are explicitly cataloged in canonical lore. The Ashlands could represent either a subduction-related volcanic arc, a continental flood basalt province over an active mantle plume, or an advective heat-pipe vent field.

### 4. Seismicity, Fault Networks, and RDA Subsurface Prospecting

Direct evidence of natural tectonic seismicity on Pandora is virtually absent from canonical text:

* **Natural Seismicity:** No canonical film scene, novel, or guide documents natural deep-focus or shallow-focus earthquakes, fault ruptures, or tsunamis. Ground vibrations shown on screen are exclusively bio-mechanical or anthropogenic.
* **Anthropogenic Seismicity:** The Resources Development Administration (RDA) utilizes massive industrial equipment that probes and fractures the upper crust. The UBE-class *Mobile Drill Platform* (MDP Alpha/Omega), deployed in the Upper Plains of the Western Frontier, operates a skyscraper-scale rotary percussion drill designed to penetrate deep bedrock to extract oil and deep mineral veins. The operation of the MDP generates high-amplitude localized ground tremors and artificial aftershocks capable of collapsing cavern systems and causing significant surface disturbance.
* **Geophysical Prospecting:** To delineate ore bodies, the RDA relies on satellite-based orbital magnetometry, gravimetry, and airborne electromagnetic surveys. While deep borehole core drilling is confirmed, official documentation provides no deep seismic reflection or refraction profiles, travel-time curves, or passive seismometer array data that would resolve crustal layering or mantle velocity structures.

### 5. Unobtanium and Economic Mineralization as Geological Objects

Unobtanium is the central economic driver of human interstellar presence on Pandora:

* **Physical Nature:** Canonically, unobtanium is a naturally occurring, room-temperature superconductor possessing an extraordinarily high critical magnetic field ($B_c$) and critical temperature ($T_c$). *James Cameron's Avatar: An Activist Survival Guide* (ASG) classifies unobtanium in prose as a complex chemical compound, although a graphic in the same volume labels it as element 120 (a synthetic superheavy element). The compound classification is the accepted lore standard.
* **Deposit Morphology:** Unobtanium occurs in massive, high-grade crystalline veins, stratiform lenses, and massive bedrock concentrations directly beneath major geological features. The richest deposits lie beneath the Omaticaya Hometree (*Kelutral*) and the Tree of Souls (*Vitraya Ramunong*), where mineral concentrations reach millions of dollars per kilogram in commercial value.
* **Terrestrial Mineralization Analogues:** On Earth, primary metallic mineral deposits require specific crustal concentration mechanisms operating over millions of years:
* *Magmatic Segregation:* Fractional crystallization and liquid immiscibility (e.g., Bushveld PGE reefs, Norilsk Ni-Cu sulfides).
* *Hydrothermal Systems:* Deep convective circulation of supercritical fluids driven by magmatic heat engines at convergent margins (e.g., porphyry Cu-Mo-Au deposits) or mid-ocean ridges (volcanogenic massive sulfides, VMS).
* *Orogenic/Metamorphic Remobilization:* Metamorphic devolatilization during continental collision that channels gold-bearing fluids into deep crustal shear zones.
If unobtanium is an epigenetic hydrothermal or magmatic deposit, its formation required sustained geothermal heat engines, high-pressure crustal fluid circulation, and deep structural conduits—features strongly associated with, though not exclusively restricted to, active plate margins.

### 6. Interior Structure and Magnetic Field Dynamics

Pandora maintains an exceptionally strong global magnetic field characterized by extreme local anomalies and flux concentrations (*Fluxcons*):

* **Core Convection and Dynamo Generation:** In terrestrial geophysics, a planetary dipole field is generated by a self-sustaining hydromagnetic dynamo operating within a rapidly rotating, convecting, electrically conducting liquid iron-nickel outer core. While Polyphemus generates an intense surrounding magnetosphere, Pandora's localized magnetic phenomena (such as the Flux Vortex) demonstrate that the moon possesses an intrinsic magnetic field interacting with magnetized crustal rocks and external Jovian-scale flux tubes.
* **Geodynamical Constraints:** A self-generated dynamo requires vigorous thermal or compositional convection in a liquid metallic core. If Pandora maintains an intrinsic dynamo, its core-mantle boundary heat flux must exceed the adiabatic core gradient, requiring an efficient mantle convection mechanism to extract core heat. Alternatively, if the field is primarily an induced magnetospheric coupling with Polyphemus, interior convective constraints are substantially relaxed. Canon does not explicitly confirm whether Pandora's metallic core is fully liquid, partially crystallized with a solid inner core, or stratified.

### 7. Hydrosphere and Crustal Hydration State

Pandora is unequivocally a water-rich world. Its surface is covered by vast liquid water oceans, dense river networks, and a hyper-humid, cloud-rich atmosphere sustained by a hyperactive hydrological cycle:

* **Mechanical Weakening via Hydration:** In terrestrial solid-Earth geophysics, water is recognized as the essential lubricating agent for mobile-lid plate tectonics. Dissolved $\mathrm{H_2O}$ enters the crystal lattices of mantle silicates, hydrolyzing Si-O bonds, which lowers mantle melting temperatures and reduces effective viscosity by multiple orders of magnitude.
* **Fault Lubrication:** Hydration of oceanic crust and the upper mantle along fault zones produces hydrous phyllosilicates (e.g., serpentine, talc, chlorite) that dramatically lower fault friction, facilitating shear localization and lithospheric failure. The presence of a massive surface ocean ensures that subducting slabs (if present) would transport vast amounts of bound water into the Pandoran mantle.

### 8. Bulk Geophysical and Orbital Parameters

Canonical literature provides explicit bulk parameters for Pandora, establishing its foundational physical profile:

* **Equatorial Radius ($R_P$):** $5,724\text{ km}$ ($0.898\,R_\oplus$; equatorial diameter $11,447\text{ km}$).
* **Planetary Mass ($M_P$):** $4.30\times 10^{24}\text{ kg}$ ($0.720\,M_\oplus$).
* **Mean Bulk Density ($\rho_P$):** Calculated directly from mass and volume:

$$\rho_P = \frac{M_P}{\frac{4}{3}\pi R_P^3} = \frac{4.300\times 10^{24}\text{ kg}}{\frac{4}{3}\pi (5.724\times 10^6\text{ m})^3} = 5,473.5\text{ kg/m}^3$$



This density is nearly identical to Earth's bulk density ($\rho_\oplus = 5,514\text{ kg/m}^3$), proving definitively that Pandora is a differentiated, iron- and silicate-dominated rocky moon with an Earth-like core-to-mantle ratio, not an icy or volatile-rich satellite.
* **Surface Gravity ($g_P$):** Calculated from Newton's law:

$$g_P = \frac{G M_P}{R_P^2} = \frac{(6.6743\times 10^{-11}\text{ m}^3\text{kg}^{-1}\text{s}^{-2})(4.300\times 10^{24}\text{ kg})}{(5.724\times 10^6\text{ m})^2} = 8.76\text{ m/s}^2 \quad (0.893\,g_\oplus)$$



*Note on Lore Variations:* Certain tie-in lore texts loosely round Pandora's surface gravity to "$\sim 0.8\,g$" or state its mass as "about half Earth's". However, using the authoritative metric diameter ($11,447\text{ km}$) and mass fraction ($0.72\,M_\oplus$) from primary tables yields an exact surface gravity of $0.893\,g$ and a mean density of $5.474\text{ g/cm}^3$.
* **Surface Area ($A_P$):** $4.117\times 10^{14}\text{ m}^2$ ($0.807\,A_\oplus$).

### 9. Systematic Analysis and Ranking of Canonical Gaps

The official Avatar canon presents distinct observational gaps regarding interior geodynamics. These gaps are categorized and ranked below by their degree of constraint on tectonic regime selection:

1. **Absence of Plate Boundary Mapping (Rank 1 - Critical):** Canon never states whether Pandora has tectonic plates, nor does it depict global spreading ridges, transform faults, or subduction trenches. *Bounded inference:* The presence of mountains and oceans permits, but does not prove, plate tectonics.
2. **Lack of Natural Seismic Records (Rank 2 - Critical):** No natural earthquake focal mechanisms, depth profiles, or Wadati-Benioff zones are documented. *Bounded inference:* Without deep-focus seismic events ($>100\text{ km}$ depth), modern subduction cannot be directly verified.
3. **Absence of Hypsometric and Crustal Thickness Data (Rank 3 - High):** No seismic Moho depth surveys or bimodal elevation distributions exist in canon. *Bounded inference:* Continental crust cannot be confirmed as petrologically felsic/granitic versus thickened mafic crust.
4. **Absence of Seafloor Age Mapping (Rank 4 - High):** No magnetic anomaly stripes or seafloor age progressions away from linear axes are recorded. *Bounded inference:* Seafloor spreading remains an unverified hypothesis.
5. **Uncertainty in Core Dynamo vs. External Induction (Rank 5 - Moderate):** The exact origin of the high-intensity magnetic anomalies remains ambiguous between core dynamo convection and Polyphemian magnetospheric induction.

## Part B - Solid-Earth Geophysics and Comparative Planetary Dynamics

### 1. The Inverse Remote Sensing Problem of Planetary Interiors

Direct physical sampling of planetary interiors is strictly limited by thermal and mechanical drilling constraints. The deepest borehole ever drilled into Earth's crust, the Kola Superdeep Borehole (SG-3), reached a terminal depth of $12,262\text{ meters}$ ($\approx 12.26\text{ km}$)—a mere $0.19\%$ of Earth's mean radius ($6,371\text{ km}$). Every model of planetary mantle and core structure is therefore an inverse problem formulated through remote geophysical constraints:

* **Mass and Mean Density:** Derived from orbital mechanics and gravitational tracking, distinguishing icy bodies ($\rho \sim 1,000\text{--}2,000\text{ kg/m}^3$) from silicate-metal rocky bodies ($\rho > 3,000\text{--}5,500\text{ kg/m}^3$).
* **Normalized Moment of Inertia Factor ($I / M R^2$ or $C / M R^2$):** Measures internal radial mass concentration. For a homogeneous solid sphere, $C/MR^2 = 0.4$. Radial concentration of mass toward the center depresses this value. Earth's factor is $0.3307$, proving a dense, central metallic core. Mars exhibits $0.365$, the Moon $0.393$, and Io $0.378$, directly constraining the radius and density of their respective metallic cores.
* **High-Pressure Mineral Physics:** Diamond anvil cell (DAC) experiments and multi-anvil press compression, coupled with synchrotron X-ray diffraction and shock-wave physics, measure equation-of-state parameters, phase transformations, and melting curves under gigapascal ($>100\text{ GPa}$) and multithousand-kelvin conditions.
* **Direct Petrological Samples:** Mantle xenoliths brought up by kimberlite eruptions sample depths up to $\sim 200\text{--}250\text{ km}$, while obducted ophiolite complexes preserve fragments of oceanic lithosphere and upper mantle.
* **Geoneutrino Flux:** Detectors (KamLAND, Borexino) measure electron antineutrinos ($\bar{\nu}_e$) emitted during the $\beta^-$-decay chains of $^{238}\mathrm{U}$ and $^{232}\mathrm{Th}$, providing an independent, non-seismic measure of planetary radiogenic heat production ($20 \pm 8\text{ TW}$ on Earth).

### 2. Elastic Wave Propagation and Seismic Moduli

Seismology is the primary tool for resolving the physical state, elastic properties, and internal layering of a planet. Seismic body waves propagate through the interior according to the equations of linear elasticity:

* **P-waves (Compressional / Primary):** Longitudinal waves involving alternating volumetric compression and rarefaction parallel to the propagation vector:

$$v_P = \sqrt{\frac{K + \frac{4}{3}\mu}{\rho}}$$



where $K$ is the adiabatic bulk modulus (incompressibility, in $\text{Pa}$), $\mu$ is the shear modulus (rigidity, in $\text{Pa}$), and $\rho$ is mass density (in $\text{kg/m}^3$).
* **S-waves (Shear / Secondary):** Transverse waves involving isochoric (constant-volume) shape deformation perpendicular to the propagation vector:

$$v_S = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{\rho}}$$



In any liquid, molten, or gaseous phase, shear stress cannot be supported elastically over macroscopic timescales, leading to the zero-rigidity criterion:


$$\mu_{\text{fluid}} \equiv 0 \implies v_S = 0 \quad \text{and} \quad v_P = \sqrt{\frac{K}{\rho}}$$


Consequently, **shear waves cannot propagate through fluids**. This physical absolute is the diagnostic that identifies magma chambers, outer cores, and subsurface oceans across the Solar System.

* **Ray Tracing and Snell's Law:** In a spherically stratified body where seismic velocity varies continuously with radius $v(r)$, Snell's law requires that the ray parameter $p$ remains constant along a ray path:

$$p = \frac{r \sin i}{v(r)} = \text{constant}$$



where $r$ is the radial distance from the planetary center, and $i$ is the angle of incidence relative to the local radial normal. Increasing elastic moduli with depth cause rays to continuously refract upward until they reach a turning point where $i = 90^\circ$ and curve back to the surface.
* **Phase Nomenclature:**
* $P, S$: Direct compressional and shear waves propagating through the mantle.
* $PP, SS$: Mantle waves reflected once at the planetary surface.
* $PcP, ScS$: Waves reflected off the core-mantle boundary (CMB).
* $PKP$ ($P'$): Compressional wave traversing the mantle and liquid outer core ($K = \text{core}$).
* $PKIKP$: Compressional wave traversing mantle, liquid outer core ($K$), solid inner core ($I$), liquid outer core, and mantle.
* $SKS$: Shear wave in the mantle converting to compressional wave in the liquid core and converting back to shear wave in the mantle.

### 3. Foundational Discoveries in Terrestrial and Extraterrestrial Seismology

The interior structure of rocky planets has been systematically revealed through seminal seismological discoveries:

* **Andrija Mohorovičić (1909) - Crust-Mantle Boundary (Moho):** Analyzing seismograms from the Kupa Valley earthquake in Croatia, Mohorovičić observed two distinct P-wave arrivals at epicentral distances $>200\text{ km}$: a direct crustal wave ($P_g$) and a faster refracted wave ($P_n$) that traveled through a deeper, high-velocity layer. This confirmed the boundary between the low-velocity silicate crust ($v_P \approx 6.0\text{--}6.8\text{ km/s}$) and the ultramafic peridotitic mantle ($v_P \approx 8.0\text{--}8.2\text{ km/s}$). The Moho averages $\sim 7\text{ km}$ depth beneath terrestrial ocean basins and $\sim 35\text{--}40\text{ km}$ (up to $70\text{ km}$ under orogenic roots) beneath continents.
* **Oldham (1906) & Gutenberg (1914) - Core-Mantle Boundary & Liquid Outer Core:** Richard Dixon Oldham recognized that delayed P-wave arrivals at large epicentral distances indicated a dense central core. Beno Gutenberg precisely determined the CMB depth at $2,890\text{ km}$ by analyzing the **P-wave shadow zone** (angular distance $\Delta \approx 103^\circ\text{--}143^\circ$, caused by strong downward refraction at the mantle-core boundary) and discovered the absolute **S-wave shadow zone** extending across the entire planetary hemisphere beyond $\Delta > 103^\circ$. Because no direct S-waves traverse the core, the outer core was proven to be liquid ($\mu = 0$).
* **Inge Lehmann (1936) - The Solid Inner Core:** Analyzing faint, high-frequency P-wave arrivals within the shadow zone ($103^\circ\text{--}143^\circ$) from New Zealand earthquakes, Lehmann proved they were reflections and refractions off a sharp velocity discontinuity at a depth of $5,150\text{ km}$. This established the presence of Earth's **solid inner core** ($R_c \approx 1,220\text{ km}$), where immense pressure ($P > 330\text{ GPa}$) solidifies iron-nickel alloy into an $\mathrm{hcp}$ crystal lattice despite temperatures exceeding $5,500\text{ K}$.
* **Mantle Phase Discontinuities ($410\text{ km}$ and $660\text{ km}$):** Mineral physics established that sharp seismic reflectors at $410\text{ km}$ and $660\text{ km}$ represent isochemical phase changes in olivine $(\mathrm{Mg,Fe})_2\mathrm{SiO}_4$:
* *$410\text{ km}$ Discontinuity:* $\alpha$-olivine transforms into wadsleyite ($\beta$-spinel structure) with a positive Clapeyron slope ($dP/dT > 0$).
* *$660\text{ km}$ Discontinuity:* $\gamma$-ringwoodite dissociates into bridgmanite $(\mathrm{Mg,Fe})\mathrm{SiO}_3$ plus ferropericlase $(\mathrm{Mg,Fe})\mathrm{O}$ with a negative Clapeyron slope ($dP/dT < 0$).
In 2014, Pearson et al. discovered a hydrous ringwoodite inclusion within an ultra-deep diamond containing $\sim 1.5\text{ wt}\%\;\mathrm{H_2O}$, confirming that Earth's mantle transition zone acts as a vast internal water reservoir.


* **Modern 3D Seismic Tomography:** Full-waveform inversions map 3D velocity anomalies ($\delta v_P, \delta v_S$), imaging subducted oceanic slabs sinking into the lower mantle and resolving two antipodal, continental-scale **Large Low-Shear-Velocity Provinces (LLSVPs)** at the CMB beneath Africa and the Pacific, flanked by Ultra-Low Velocity Zones (ULVZs).
* **Extraterrestrial Seismology:**
* *Apollo Passive Seismic Experiment (PSE, 1969–1977):* Deployed four seismometers on the Moon, recording over 12,000 events (deep tidal moonquakes at $700\text{--}1,200\text{ km}$ depth, shallow tectonic moonquakes, and impacts). PSE measured a lunar crustal thickness of $\sim 30\text{--}40\text{ km}$ and identified a small metallic core ($R_c \sim 330\text{ km}$).
* *NASA InSight Mission on Mars (SEIS, 2018–2022):* The SEIS instrument detected $>1,300$ marsquakes, providing the first in-situ seismic measurement of a single-plate planetary interior:
* Martian crustal thickness: $20\text{--}39\text{ km}$ beneath the lander; global average modeled at $24\text{--}72\text{ km}$.
* Martian core radius: $1,830 \pm 40\text{ km}$, proving a large, low-density, fully liquid metallic core rich in light sulfur/carbon/oxygen elements (Stähler et al. 2021; Knapmeyer-Endrun et al. 2021).
* Tectonic regime: InSight confirmed Mars operates in a classic **stagnant lid** regime, with seismicity focused along cooling extensional fault provinces (Cerberus Fossae) and no plate boundaries.

### 4. Planetary Thermal Budgets, Rheological Stratification, and Viscous Creep

Earth loses internal heat at a measured global rate of $47 \pm 2\text{ TW}$, corresponding to a mean surface heat flux of $q = 92.1\text{ mW/m}^2$ ($0.092\text{ W/m}^2$). This flux is partitioned into oceanic heat flow ($\sim 32\text{ TW}$, average flux $\approx 100\text{ mW/m}^2$), dominated by young seafloor cooling and hydrothermal venting, and continental heat flow ($\sim 15\text{ TW}$, average flux $\approx 65\text{ mW/m}^2$), sustained largely by crustal radiogenic decay ($^{238}\mathrm{U}$, $^{235}\mathrm{U}$, $^{232}\mathrm{Th}$, $^{40}\mathrm{K}$).

* **Thermal Gradients:** In conducting thermal boundary layers (lithosphere and the CMB $D''$ layer), temperature increases steeply with depth ($\sim 15\text{--}30\text{ K/km}$). In the convecting mantle interior, efficient solid-state advection homogenizes entropy, forcing the temperature gradient to follow the isentrope (convective adiabat):

$$\left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial z}\right)_S = \frac{\alpha g T}{C_p} \approx 0.3\text{--}0.5\text{ K/km}$$



where $\alpha$ is the volumetric thermal expansion coefficient ($\sim 2\text{--}3\times 10^{-5}\text{ K}^{-1}$) and $C_p$ is specific heat capacity ($\sim 1.2\times 10^3\text{ J}\cdot\text{kg}^{-1}\text{K}^{-1}$).
* **Compositional vs. Mechanical Layering:**
* *Compositional Layers:* Defined by chemistry—**Crust** (felsic-to-mafic silicates, $\rho \sim 2,700\text{--}3,000\text{ kg/m}^3$), **Mantle** (ultramafic peridotite/bridgmanite, $\rho \sim 3,300\text{--}5,500\text{ kg/m}^3$), and **Core** (Fe-Ni alloy, $\rho \sim 9,900\text{--}13,000\text{ kg/m}^3$).
* *Mechanical Layers:* Defined by rheological response—**Lithosphere** (cold, rigid, elastic boundary layer that deforms by brittle faulting or elastic flexing), **Asthenosphere** (warm, ductile solid mantle undergoing solid-state creep, melt fraction $\ll 1\%$), and **Mesosphere** (high-pressure ductile lower mantle).


* **Viscous Creep Mechanics:** Silicate mantle rock flows as a non-Newtonian visco-plastic solid via solid-state crystalline defect motion:
* *Diffusion Creep (Newtonian, linear stress dependence):* Vacancy migration through crystal grains (Nabarro-Herring creep) or along grain boundaries (Coble creep).
* *Dislocation Creep (Non-Newtonian, power-law $\dot{\epsilon} \propto \sigma^n$ with $n \approx 3\text{--}3.5$):* Dislocation glide and climb along crystal planes, dominant in the upper mantle.


* **Arrhenius Temperature Dependence:** Effective mantle viscosity $\eta$ varies exponentially with temperature:

$$\eta(T, P) = \eta_0 \exp\left(\frac{E_a + P V_a}{R_g T}\right)$$



where $E_a$ is activation energy ($\sim 240\text{--}500\text{ kJ/mol}$), $V_a$ is activation volume ($\sim 10^{-5}\text{ m}^3/\text{mol}$), $R_g$ is the universal gas constant ($8.314\text{ J}\cdot\text{mol}^{-1}\text{K}^{-1}$), $P$ is pressure, and $T$ is absolute temperature. A temperature change of only $\sim 200\text{ K}$ alters mantle viscosity by 3 to 4 orders of magnitude. Postglacial isostatic rebound and satellite geoid constraints establish that Earth's upper mantle viscosity is $\eta_{\text{upper}} \sim 10^{19}\text{--}10^{21}\text{ Pa}\cdot\text{s}$, while the lower mantle is $\eta_{\text{lower}} \sim 10^{21}\text{--}10^{23}\text{ Pa}\cdot\text{s}$.

### 5. Solid-State Mantle Convection, Scaling Laws, and Lithospheric Force Balances

Mantle convection is the fundamental thermodynamic engine transporting planetary heat outward. The onset and vigor of thermal convection are governed by the dimensionless **Rayleigh Number ($\mathrm{Ra}$)**:

$$\mathrm{Ra} = \frac{\rho g \alpha \Delta T d^3}{\eta \kappa}$$

* $\rho$: Mantle reference density ($\text{kg/m}^3$, Earth $\sim 4,000\text{ kg/m}^3$)
* $g$: Acceleration due to gravity ($\text{m/s}^2$, Earth $\approx 9.81\text{ m/s}^2$)
* $\alpha$: Volumetric thermal expansion coefficient ($\text{K}^{-1}$, $\sim 2.5\times 10^{-5}\text{ K}^{-1}$)
* $\Delta T$: Superadiabatic temperature difference across the mantle ($\text{K}$, $\sim 2,000\text{--}3,000\text{ K}$)
* $d$: Mantle convective layer thickness ($\text{m}$, Earth $\approx 2.89\times 10^6\text{ m}$)
* $\eta$: Dynamic mantle viscosity ($\text{Pa}\cdot\text{s}$, $\sim 10^{21}\text{ Pa}\cdot\text{s}$)
* $\kappa$: Thermal diffusivity ($\text{m}^2/\text{s}$, $\kappa = \frac{k}{\rho C_p} \sim 10^{-6}\text{ m}^2/\text{s}$)

The critical Rayleigh number for the onset of convection is $\mathrm{Ra}_c \sim 10^3$ ($\mathrm{Ra}_c = 657.5$ for two free-slip boundaries). Earth's mantle operates at $\mathrm{Ra} \sim 10^6\text{--}10^8$, driving turbulent, time-dependent solid-state convection.

* **Nusselt Number Scaling:** The efficiency of convective heat transport over pure conduction is given by:

$$\mathrm{Nu} = \frac{q_{\text{surface}}}{q_{\text{conductive}}} \propto \mathrm{Ra}^\beta$$



where $\beta \approx \frac{1}{3}$. This non-linear feedback forms a planetary self-regulating thermostat: if mantle temperature rises, viscosity $\eta$ drops exponentially, driving $\mathrm{Ra}$ upward, which increases convective vigor ($\mathrm{Nu}$) and accelerates heat loss until the mantle cools back to equilibrium.
* **Plate as the Upper Thermal Boundary Layer:** Tectonic plates are not passive rafts propelled by mantle conveyor belts; the plate is the cold, rigid upper boundary layer of the convection system. Conductive cooling from the surface into the lithosphere follows 1D half-space cooling:

$$T(z, t) = T_s + (T_m - T_s) \operatorname{erf}\left(\frac{z}{2\sqrt{\kappa t}}\right)$$



where $t$ is crustal age, $T_s$ is surface temperature, and $T_m$ is mantle temperature. This yields definitive square-root-of-age scaling laws:
* *Lithospheric Thickness:* $h_L(t) \approx 2.32\sqrt{\kappa t} \propto \sqrt{t}$
* *Seafloor Bathymetric Subsidence:* $d(t) = d_0 + C_d \sqrt{t}$ (where $C_d \approx 350\text{ m}\cdot\text{Ma}^{-1/2}$)
* *Conductive Heat Flux:* $q(t) = \frac{k (T_m - T_s)}{\sqrt{\pi \kappa t}} \propto \frac{1}{\sqrt{t}}$


* **Lithospheric Force Balance:**
1. **Slab Pull ($F_{SP}$):** The dominant driving force ($>80\text{--}90\%$ of net torque). Oceanic lithosphere becomes denser than the underlying asthenosphere after $\sim 20\text{--}30\text{ Ma}$. Upon subduction, the metamorphic conversion of basalt/gabbro to ultra-dense eclogite ($\Delta \rho \approx +400\text{ kg/m}^3$) provides a massive negative buoyancy force pulling the plate down.
2. **Ridge Push ($F_{RP}$):** A secondary body force caused by the gravitational sliding of elevated, buoyant lithosphere at mid-ocean ridges down the bathymetric slope away from the spreading axis.
3. **Resisting Forces:** Viscous plate bending at subduction trenches, frictional shear resistance along the subduction interplate contact, and asthenospheric basal drag resistance ($F_D$).
A plate's absolute surface velocity correlates directly with the perimeter length of subducting slab attached to it.

### 6. Kinematics and Geodynamic Manifestations of Mobile-Lid Tectonics

The modern paradigm of terrestrial plate tectonics integrates structural geology, geophysics, and petrology:

* **Historical Development:** Alfred Wegener's continental drift hypothesis lacked a viable physical mechanism. Arthur Holmes proposed solid-state mantle convection. Marie Tharp and Bruce Heezen mapped the mid-ocean ridge system, leading Harry Hess and Robert Dietz to formulate seafloor spreading.
* **The Decisive Evidence Chain:**
* *Vine-Matthews-Morley Hypothesis:* Basaltic magma erupting at mid-ocean ridges records geomagnetic reversals as it cools below the Curie point, creating symmetrical marine magnetic stripes.
* *Crustal Age Distribution:* Deep-sea drilling proved oceanic crust ages monotonically away from ridges; **no oceanic crust anywhere on Earth is older than $\sim 180\text{--}200\text{ Ma}$**, whereas continental cratons exceed $3.8\text{ Ga}$.
* *Wadati-Benioff Zones:* Dipping planar zones of earthquakes extending down to $670\text{ km}$ trace cold, rigid slabs undergoing dehydration embrittlement.
* *Space Geodesy:* Satellite GNSS/GPS measures surface plate motions of $1\text{--}15\text{ cm/year}$, precisely matching geological spreading rates.


* **Isostasy and Crustal Roots:** Surface topography is buoyantly compensated by low-density crustal roots. Under Airy isostasy, topographic elevation $h$ is supported by a crustal root $r$:

$$r = h \left(\frac{\rho_c}{\rho_m - \rho_c}\right)$$



where $\rho_c$ is crustal density ($\sim 2,800\text{ kg/m}^3$) and $\rho_m$ is mantle density ($\sim 3,300\text{ kg/m}^3$). A $5\text{ km}$-high mountain range requires a root of roughly $28\text{ km}$ extending into the mantle.
* **Petrological Origin of Continental Crust:** Continental crust is intermediate-to-felsic (andesitic-to-granitic, $\mathrm{SiO_2} \ge 60\text{ wt}\%$) and buoyant ($\rho \approx 2,700\text{ kg/m}^3$). Granite cannot be generated in significant volume by direct dry partial melting of peridotitic mantle; it requires **hydrous partial remelting of basaltic crust within subduction zone magmatic arcs**. Continental landmasses are therefore chemical and hydrological proxies for liquid water and subduction-driven recycling.

### 7. The Convective Regime Spectrum in Comparative Planetology

In comparative planetology, a rocky planet does not arbitrarily possess or lack tectonics. Mantle convection organizes into distinct regimes governed by boundary conditions and lithospheric yield strength ($\sigma_y$):
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

| CONVECTIVE REGIME SPECTRUM |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

| Mobile Lid (Earth) | Stagnant Lid (Venus, Mars) | Heat Pipe (Io, Early Earth) |
| Convective stress > | Convective stress < | Extreme tidal/internal heat |
| yield strength. Slabs | yield strength. Single | advected via melt pipes. |
| subduct and recycle. | rigid lid; heat escapes | Volcanic burial produces |
| Bimodal hypsometry. | by slow conduction. | deep hoop compression. |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

```

#### Mobile-Lid Regime (Earth)
The lithosphere fails under convective stresses ($\sigma_{\text{convective}} > \sigma_y$). The cold boundary layer fractures into distinct plates, bends, subducts into the asthenosphere, and is fully recycled.
*   *Requirements:* Low lithospheric yield strength ($\sigma_y < 100\text{--}200\text{ MPa}$), water-assisted fault weakening (serpentinization, pore fluid overpressure), low fault friction ($\mu_f < 0.2$), and moderate surface temperatures.
*   *The Initiation Problem:* Pristine, unbroken lithosphere has an intrinsic yield strength exceeding $1\text{ GPa}$, far higher than typical convective stresses ($\sim 10\text{--}100\text{ MPa}$). Breaking an intact stagnant lid to initiate plate tectonics requires structural damage accumulation, thermal shear localization, or external catastrophic triggers (e.g., bolide impacts or mantle plume impingement). Consequently, the timing of plate tectonic onset on Earth remains hotly debated in geochemistry and geodynamics:
    *   *Hadean/Early Archean ($>3.8\text{ Ga}$):* Argued from detrital Jack Hills zircon trace elements (Ti-thermometry suggesting low-temperature hydrous granitic melts) and inclusions.
    *   *Meso-to-Neoarchean ($3.0\text{--}2.5\text{ Ga}$):* Argued from the global appearance of paired metamorphic belts, potassic granites, and modern cratonic keels.
    *   *Neoproterozoic ($<1.0\text{--}0.8\text{ Ga}$):* Argued by Stern and others from the strict geological absence of modern subduction index rocks—**blueschists, lawsonite-bearing metamorphic suites, and ultra-high-pressure (UHP) coesite/diamond eclogites**—prior to $\sim 800\text{ Ma}$, reflecting the cooling of Earth's mantle below critical subduction geotherms.

#### Stagnant-Lid Regime (Venus, Mars, Mercury, Moon)
Convective stresses are insufficient to overcome lithospheric yield strength ($\sigma_{\text{convective}} < \sigma_y$). The planet is encased in a single, rigid, immobile global shell. Heat escapes primarily by slow solid-state conduction through the lid, augmented by localized volcanic hot spots:
*   *Venus:* Nearly identical to Earth in radius ($0.95\,R_\oplus$), mass ($0.815\,M_\oplus$), and bulk radiogenic inventory. Yet Magellan radar imagery proved Venus lacks a global mid-ocean ridge system, plate mosaics, or subduction networks. Its surface displays $\sim 1,000$ impact craters distributed randomly, yielding a young average surface age of $\sim 300\text{--}750\text{ Ma}$. Explanations for stagnant-lid behavior on Venus include:
    1.  *Complete Dehydration:* The runaway greenhouse boiled away surface water, desiccating the lithosphere. Without water, silicate rocks are extremely strong, preventing shear localization and fault lubrication.
    2.  *High Surface Temperature ($465^\circ\text{C}$):* Elevates the basal ductile transition, suppressing lithospheric thermal stresses.
    *Dynamic Modes:* Venus may undergo **episodic global overturns** (catastrophic stagnant-lid failure when thermal accumulation thins the lid until it founders globally) or a modern **squishy-lid regime** characterized by localized lithospheric dripping and plume-driven corona tectonics.
*   *Mars:* A classic stagnant-lid world. Because the lithosphere never moves across the underlying mantle, long-lived stationary mantle plumes built colossal volcanic constructs over billions of years (Olympus Mons, $22\text{ km}$ high, $600\text{ km}$ across; Tharsis rise). Extensional stress from mantle upwelling generated Valles Marineris ($4,000\text{ km}$ long) as an aborted rift without seafloor spreading.

#### Sluggish-Lid Regime
An intermediate state where high mantle temperatures and strongly temperature-dependent viscosity allow slow, highly viscous deformation of the surface lid without localized brittle plate boundaries.

#### Heat-Pipe (Advective Resurfacing) Regime (Io, Early Earth)
When internal heat production is exceptionally high ($q \gg 0.5\text{--}1.0\text{ W/m}^2$), convective heat transport transitions from conductive lithospheric loss to **advective volcanic piping** (Moore & Webb, 2013):
*   *Physics of the Heat Pipe:* Mantle melting is continuous and voluminous. Magma segregates rapidly and ascends through narrow, cold, vertical lithospheric conduits, erupting directly onto the planetary surface.
*   *Downwelling and Burial Advection:* Heat is lost to space instantly as erupted lavas freeze at the surface. The continuous accumulation of newly erupted volcanic strata forces older surface layers to **subside downward**. The lithospheric temperature profile is governed by the 1D heat-pipe advective equation:
    $$T(z) = T_0 + (T_a - T_0) \left[\frac{\exp(z/l) - 1}{\exp(d_L/l) - 1}\right]$$
    where $l = \kappa / v_{\text{advection}}$ is the characteristic advective length scale ($v_{\text{advection}}$ is the downward burial velocity) and $d_L$ is lithospheric thickness.
*   *Paradoxical Thermal State:* Rapid downward advection of cold surface rocks keeps the lithosphere **exceptionally cold, thick, and rigid** ($d_L > 20\text{--}50\text{ km}$) despite extreme internal mantle melting.
*   *Io Case Study:* Jupiter's moon Io vents a tidal heat budget of $\sim 100\text{ TW}$ ($q \approx 2.4\text{ W/m}^2$, over 25 times Earth's heat flux). Io displays $>400$ active volcanic centers (paterae) and resurfaces at rates of $\sim 1\text{ cm/year}$, wiping out all impact craters.
*   *Non-Tectonic Mountain Uplift Mechanism (Bland & McKinnon, 2016):* Io possesses over 100 non-volcanic mountains rising up to $17.5\text{ km}$ in height (e.g., Boösaule Montes). **These mountains are not collisional orogens from plate tectonics**. As erupted strata subside downward on a spherical body, the surface area shrinks as a function of the radius squared:
    $$\Delta A \propto \Delta (R^2)$$
    This geometric constriction induces immense horizontal compressive hoop stresses ($\sigma_{\theta\theta} > 1\text{ GPa}$) in the subsiding crust. When combined with deep crustal thermal expansion near the base of the lithosphere, the lithosphere fails along deep, through-going **thrust faults**, forcing colossal crustal fault blocks upward. **Io proves that an intensely tidally heated rocky world can build towering mountain ranges without plate tectonics**.

#### Super-Earth Tectonics Debate
Geodynamicists actively debate whether increasing planetary mass favors or suppresses mobile-lid tectonics:
*   *Valencia et al. (2007):* Argue that shear stress driving mantle convection scales as $\tau \propto M^{0.28}$, while lithospheric thickness scales as $h \propto M^{-0.16}$, making plate failure and subduction **inevitable on super-Earths**.
*   *O'Neill & Lenardic (2007) and Korenaga (2010):* Argue that higher internal gravity increases fault friction and lithospheric yield stress via hydrostatic confinement ($P = \rho g z$), while convective stresses do not increase commensurately, driving massive super-Earths into **episodic or stagnant-lid regimes**.

### 9. Economic Ore Genesis as a Function of Crustal Mobility

On Earth, the physical concentration of trace elements from parts-per-million ($\text{ppm}$) or parts-per-billion ($\text{ppb}$) background abundances to minable ore grades (enrichment factors of $10^2$ to $10^5$) is intimately linked to tectonic and hydrological recycling:

*   **Magmatic Segregation:** Fractional crystallization and liquid immiscibility in mafic/ultramafic layered intrusions concentrate chromium, platinum-group elements (PGEs, e.g., Bushveld Merensky Reef), and nickel-copper sulfides (Norilsk-Talnakh, Sudbury). This mechanism does not strictly require plate boundaries and can operate in intraplate continental flood basalts or large igneous provinces on single-plate worlds.
*   **Hydrothermal Systems at Active Margins:** Deep convective circulation of aqueous fluids extracts and concentrates metals. Porphyry copper-molybdenum-gold deposits form in magmatic arcs where water released by subducting slab dehydration exsolves from ascending intermediate magmas, hydrofracturing upper-crustal rocks. Volcanogenic massive sulfide (VMS) deposits form along mid-ocean ridge spreading axes where circulating seawater leaches metals from basaltic crust and precipitates massive sulfides at black smoker vents. Orogenic gold systems form during collisional orogeny when regional metamorphic devolatilization channels fluids along deep crustal fault networks.
*   **Sedimentary and Surficial Systems:** Banded Iron Formations (BIFs) and laterites require long-lived hydrological cycles, atmospheric redox transitions, and sustained chemical weathering of uplifting mountain belts.
*   **Ore Genesis on Single-Plate and Heat-Pipe Bodies:** On stagnant-lid worlds like the Moon and Mars, hydrothermal mineralization is strictly localized and short-lived, driven only by impact cratering or isolated volcanic edifices. Neither world possesses regional porphyry belts, VMS horizons, or orogenic gold belts due to the absence of continuous subduction-driven fluid fluxing. On a heat-pipe world like Io, extreme volcanic advection produces massive sublimate deposits and sulfur segregations, but the total absence of water precludes all hydrous hydrothermal leaching and porphyry-style mineralization.

### 10. Foundational Open Problems in Solid-Earth Geophysics

Several fundamental geodynamical questions remain active subjects of scientific investigation:

1.  **Onset Timing of Plate Tectonics:** Hypotheses range across the Hadean ($>4.0\text{ Ga}$), Meso-to-Neoarchean ($3.0\text{--}2.5\text{ Ga}$), and Neoproterozoic ($<1.0\text{ Ga}$).
2.  **Tectonic Regime of Venus:** Active debate between a fully stagnant lid, episodic catastrophic global overturns, and continuous localized "squishy-lid" corona subduction.
3.  **Subduction Initiation Mechanics:** Spontaneous breaking of pristine, unbroken lithosphere requires localized damage accumulation and shear softening mechanisms that remain difficult to reproduce in self-consistent numerical models.
4.  **Compositional Nature of LLSVPs:** Ambiguity remains as to whether mantle LLSVPs represent purely thermal upwellings or chemically distinct, dense piles of subducted primordial crust.
5.  **Mantle Volatile Storage Capacity:** Estimates of the total mantle water inventory in the transition zone and lower mantle range from 0.5 to over 4 terrestrial ocean equivalents.

### 2. Tiered Geodynamical Hypotheses

Evaluating Pandora's geodynamic state requires testing competing convective regimes against canonical observations, mechanical viability, and observational discriminants:

#### Hypothesis 1 (H1): High-Flux Mobile Lid (Fast-Spreading Plate Tectonics)
*   *Mechanism:* Pandora operates in a mobile-lid convective regime. The high internal heat budget ($100\text{--}300\text{ TW}$) is evacuated by ultra-rapid seafloor spreading ($v_p \sim 15\text{--}30\text{ cm/year}$). Abundant surface water continuously hydrates oceanic crust, maintaining low fault yield strength ($\sigma_y < 100\text{ MPa}$) and enabling continuous subduction.
*   *Predicted Features:* Linear mid-ocean spreading ridges, extensive island-arc chains, deep oceanic trenches, collisional orogens with granitic crustal roots, rich hydrothermal porphyry and VMS ore deposits, and bimodal hypsometry.
*   *Canon Support:* Explains the presence of liquid oceans, rugged mountain belts, massive hydrothermal ore bodies (unobtanium), and biogenic atoll archipelagos.
*   *Canon Strain:* High heat flow implies that oceanic lithosphere remains young, thin, and warm, reducing negative thermal buoyancy ($F_{SP}$) and making slabs prone to tearing or break-off. Additionally, no canonical plate boundaries or subduction trenches are officially mapped.

#### Hypothesis 2 (H2): Advective Heat-Pipe Regime (Io Analogue)
*   *Mechanism:* Pandora loses its tidal heat budget almost entirely through advective volcanic piping (Moore & Webb, 2013). Magma ascends through vertical conduits to erupt across volcanic provinces like the Ashlands. Continuous volcanic burial forces the crust downward, where geometric surface area reduction ($\Delta A \propto \Delta R^2$) generates massive lateral compressive hoop stresses. The lithosphere fails along deep thrust faults, uplifting colossal fault-block mountains without plate tectonics (Bland & McKinnon, 2016).
*   *Predicted Features:* Distributed volcanic centers, zero impact craters, unimodal hypsometry with high-standing thrust blocks, predominantly basaltic/ultramafic crust, and magmatic segregation/sublimate ore bodies.
*   *Canon Support:* Directly accounts for hundreds of terawatts of tidal dissipation; explains towering mountain relief in a tidally heated moon without requiring plate boundaries; matches the presence of explosive volcanic wastelands.
*   *Canon Strain:* Rapid, pervasive volcanic resurfacing ($\sim\text{mm to cm/year}$) would bury, sterilize, and destroy terrestrial ecosystems. This is difficult to reconcile with an ancient, stable, highly interconnected global neural biosphere (Eywa) whose sacred sites (e.g., Tree of Souls) have existed undisturbed for thousands of years in deep, well-developed soils.

#### Hypothesis 3 (H3): Plume-Dominated Stagnant Lid
*   *Mechanism:* Convective stresses fail to break the Pandoran lithosphere. Pandora is a single-plate body. The high heat budget is transported via conduction through a thin stagnant lid augmented by colossal mantle plumes and localized extensional rifting.
*   *Predicted Features:* Massive solitary shield volcanoes (Martian Tharsis style), colossal rift chasmata (Valles Marineris style), unimodal hypsometry, and absence of granitic continental crust.
*   *Canon Support:* Modal convective regime for rocky bodies in the Solar System (Mercury, Moon, Mars, Venus).
*   *Canon Strain:* Severe. Canon shows no colossal Olympus Mons-scale volcanic shields. Stagnant-lid regimes struggle to generate granitic continental masses or widespread arc-type hydrothermal ore concentrations (unobtanium).

#### Hypothesis 4 (H4): Hybrid / Episodic "Squishy-Lid" Regime
*   *Mechanism:* A hybrid tectonic mode where high internal heat and water produce a rheologically soft, deformable lithosphere. Rather than rigid plates, deformation is accommodated by localized lithospheric dripping (delamination), plume-induced mobile belts, and peeling-back subduction around volcanic provinces, separated by long quiescent intervals.
*   *Predicted Features:* Micro-continents, greenstone-belt-style granite-greenstone terrains, localized volcanic arcs, and hybrid hypsometry.
*   *Canon Support & Synthesis:* Reconciles the presence of an ancient, ecologically stable biosphere with a high tidal heat budget. Volcanic resurfacing is regionally restricted (e.g., the Ashlands), preserving ancient rainforest biomes while allowing vigorous mantle cooling and localized mountain building.

### 3. Prioritized Geophysical Exploration Architecture

To definitively resolve Pandora's convective regime, an expeditionary scientific program would deploy the following prioritized geophysical instruments:

1.  **Landed Broadband Seismometer Array (Primary Discriminant):** Tripartite array operating for $>1$ orbital cycle. The detection of dipping Wadati-Benioff zones ($>100\text{--}300\text{ km}$ depth) proves active subduction (H1). Absence of deep events and presence of shallow thrust/extensional events confirms H2 or H3. Core-refracted phases ($PKP, PKIKP$) determine core radius and dynamo state.
2.  **Orbital Radar Altimetry and Gravimetry (Secondary Discriminant):** Measures global hypsometry. A strictly bimodal distribution proves two compositionally distinct crustal types (H1). A unimodal distribution with elevated tails confirms a single-crust stagnant or heat-pipe regime (H2/H3).
3.  **Orbital Crater Size-Frequency Distribution (CSFD) Mapping:** A surface completely devoid of impact craters proves continuous, hyperactive global resurfacing ($>1\text{ mm/year}$), confirming H2. A crater-free ocean floor bordering cratered continental shields ($>1\text{ Ga}$) confirms H1.
4.  **Marine Aeromagnetometry:** Symmetrical, linear magnetic reversal stripes parallel to marine rifts definitively prove seafloor spreading (H1). Irregular, non-linear magnetic fields confirm H2 or external induction.
5.  **Metamorphic Petrology and Geochemical Sampling:** Discovery of glaucophane-bearing blueschists, jadeite + quartz, or coesite-bearing eclogites proves cold subduction-zone metamorphic geotherms ($P/T < 10^\circ\text{C/km}$), providing indisputable petrological proof of plate tectonics (H1).

1.  **The Remote Sensing Imperative:** The Kola Superdeep Borehole ($12.26\text{ km}$) penetrated $<0.2\%$ of Earth's radius; solid-body interiors are entirely remotely sensed via mass, moments of inertia, and seismology.
2.  **The Zero-Rigidity Criterion:** Fluids have $\mu \equiv 0$, preventing shear wave propagation ($v_S = \sqrt{\mu/\rho} = 0$). Gutenberg's $103^\circ$ S-wave shadow zone is the primary proof of a liquid outer core.
3.  **The Lehmann Precursor Discovery (1936):** Faint P-wave arrivals within the shadow zone ($PKIKP$) proved the existence of Earth's solid inner core at $5,150\text{ km}$ depth.
4.  **Extraterrestrial Seismology Precedent:** Apollo seismometers revealed deep lunar moonquakes; NASA InSight proved Mars has an average crustal thickness of $24\text{--}72\text{ km}$ and a large liquid core ($1,830\pm 40\text{ km}$) beneath a stagnant lid.
5.  **Mantle Convective Rayleigh Number:** $\mathrm{Ra} = \frac{\rho g \alpha \Delta T d^3}{\eta \kappa}$. Earth operates at $\mathrm{Ra} \sim 10^6\text{--}10^8$; Pandora's high internal heating yields $\mathrm{Ra} \sim 10^7\text{--}10^8$, forcing vigorous convection.
6.  **The Plate as the Boundary Layer:** Tectonic plates are not passive rafts; they are the cold, rigid upper thermal boundary layer of the mantle convection system.
7.  **Square-Root-of-Age Scaling:** Conductive cooling dictates oceanic lithosphere thickness $h(t) \propto \sqrt{t}$, seafloor subsidence $d(t) \propto \sqrt{t}$, and conductive heat flux $q(t) \propto 1/\sqrt{t}$.
8.  **Tectonic Force Dominance:** Slab pull ($F_{SP}$) drives $>80\%$ of plate motion; plate velocity correlates directly with the perimeter of attached subducting slab.
9.  **The Seafloor Age Paradox:** No terrestrial ocean crust exceeds $\sim 200\text{ Ma}$, whereas continental cratons exceed $3.8\text{ Ga}$, proving continuous subduction recycling.
10. **The Subduction Index Rocks:** Modern plate tectonics is petrologically indexed by high-pressure, low-temperature metamorphic rocks (**blueschists and coesite-bearing eclogites**), which only appear widely in Earth's geological record after $\sim 0.8\text{--}1.0\text{ Ga}$.
11. **Granite as a Tectonic/Hydrological Statement:** True continental crust (granite/andesite) cannot form by dry mantle melting; it requires hydrous remelting of basaltic crust in subduction zones.
12. **The Io Heat-Pipe Mountain Paradox:** Io generates $>100\text{ TW}$ ($q \approx 2.4\text{ W/m}^2$). Downward volcanic burial shrinks crustal area ($\Delta A \propto \Delta R^2$), generating massive horizontal hoop compression that builds $17\text{ km}$-tall thrust-block mountains without plate tectonics (Bland & McKinnon, 2016).
13. **Pandora Bulk Geodesy:** Mass $M = 4.30\times 10^{24}\text{ kg}$ ($0.72\,M_\oplus$), Radius $R = 5,724\text{ km}$, Density $\rho = 5,473.5\text{ kg/m}^3$, Surface Gravity $g = 8.76\text{ m/s}^2$ ($0.893\,g$). Pandora is a fully differentiated rocky silicate/iron moon.
14. **Pandora Surface Heat Flux Range:** Dissipating a $100\text{--}300\text{ TW}$ tidal heat budget across Pandora's $4.12\times 10^{14}\text{ m}^2$ surface yields $q = 0.24\text{--}0.73\text{ W/m}^2$ ($3\text{--}8\times$ Earth's flux).
15. **The Unobtanium Petrogenetic Tension:** High-grade hydrothermal ore deposits require long-lived magmatic fluid circulation cells typical of convergent plate boundaries.
16. **The Biosphere vs. Heat-Pipe Incompatibility:** A pure Io-style heat pipe resurfaces at centimeters per year, which contradicts Pandora's ancient, stable, continuous global neural ecosystem (Eywa).
17. **The Hybrid Solution (H4 - Squishy Lid):** High heat and lithospheric water may drive Pandora into a deformable, squishy-lid or episodic regime, localizing violent volcanism (Ashlands) while preserving ancient rainforest cratons.
18. **The Decisive Test:** A three-station broadband seismometer network is the ultimate scientific instrument to resolve Wadati-Benioff zones, Moho depth, and core state, settling Pandora's tectonic regime conclusively.
```
