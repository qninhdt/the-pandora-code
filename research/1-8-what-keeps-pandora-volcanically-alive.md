### The Mangkwan Clan and the Ashlands Catastrophe

The Mangkwan clan, colloquially designated the "Ash People," represents a profound sociopolitical and ecological divergence within Pandoran ethnography. Led by Varang, who uniquely consolidates the twin leadership roles of *Olo'eykte* (clan leader) and *Tsahìk* (spiritual matriarch), the Mangkwan inhabit the Ashlands—a desolate, volcanic biome structured around the remnants of a destroyed ancient rainforest.

The catastrophe caused not only physical devastation but an existential spiritual rupture. During the eruption, the clan’s localized neural interface was severed by thermal and physical destruction. When intercessory prayers failed to halt the advancing pyroclastic flows and ash clouds ("Eywa did not come"), the surviving Mangkwan under Varang rejected traditional Na'vi mores. ### Surface Volcanism, Geothermal Activity, and Crustal Dynamism

Pandora is an active terrestrial moon characterized by high rates of continental drift, pervasive volcanism, and globally distributed geothermal networks. Official franchise publications, notably *James Cameron's Avatar: An Activist Survival Guide* and Stephen Baxter's *The Science of Avatar*, detail a planetary crust defined by intense tectonism.

Unlike the stable continental shields that characterize geologically mature terrestrial worlds, Pandora's lithosphere is fragmented into smaller, rapidly moving tectonic plates. This accelerated tectonic regime prevents the formation of massive, supercontinental landmasses, favoring instead island archipelagos, shallow epicontinental seaways, and rugged volcanic cordilleras. Crustal plate boundaries are outlined by chains of active composite volcanoes, extensive rift systems, and high-temperature hydrothermal fields. The presence of widespread flood basalts, geothermal geysers, and mineral-dense volcanic arcs demonstrates that mantle-driven thermal transport on Pandora is a continuous, moon-wide phenomenon rather than an isolated intraplate anomaly.

### The Magnetic Environment and Unobtanium: Deconstructing the "Magnetic Heating" Trope

The defining geochemical and physical marker of Pandora is the room-temperature superconducting mineral **unobtanium**. The *Activist Survival Guide* describes unobtanium as a naturally occurring quasicrystalline mineral characterized by a stable atomic lattice exhibiting non-repeating fivefold symmetry. This lattice structure gives the material high mechanical strength and an elevated critical transition temperature:

$$T_c \approx 1116^\circ\text{C} \quad (1389\text{ K})$$

The mineral also possesses an upper critical magnetic field limit ($H_{c2}$) exceeding $10^9\text{ Gauss}$ ($10^5\text{ Tesla}$).

Within regions of high crustal concentration, such as the *Mons Veritatis* (the Hallelujah Mountains), unobtanium ore beds interact with the magnetosphere of Polyphemus and Pandora's core dynamo. Through the Meissner-Ochsenfeld effect and magnetic flux pinning, these deposits generate macroscopic magnetic levitation forces that suspend mountain-sized silicate-superconductor monoliths within localized magnetic flux gradients.

In popular science discussions and fan communities, these magnetic phenomena are often misidentified as the primary thermal engine driving Pandora's active magma production. However, official canon makes no such claim. The canonical formation history in the *Activist Survival Guide* states:

> "Billions of years ago, when the planets and satellites of the Alpha Centauri system were condensing from the primordial stellar nebula, a Mars-sized planetesimal may have crashed into the still-molten Pandora... The high temperatures and pressures produced in the collision far exceeded anything wrought by human technology. These forces interacted with Polyphemus's intense magnetic field..."

The lore establishes that intense primordial heat and impact shocks allowed the superconducting mineral to crystallize out of a molten silicate-metallic liquid in the presence of an external magnetic field. Canon does not state that magnetism acts as a continuous internal heat source. The magnetic field governs levitation dynamics, flux vortices, and atmospheric auroral storms, but it does not supply the ongoing megawattage required to melt Pandora's silicate mantle.

### Interior Structure and Geochemical Composition

Pandora's bulk density ($\rho \approx 5.46\text{ g/cm}^3$) closely mirrors Earth's density ($\rho_\oplus = 5.515\text{ g/cm}^3$), indicating a fully differentiated terrestrial body with a dense metallic core and a thick silicate mantle. The metallic core consists primarily of nickel-iron alloys and comprises approximately 28% to 32% of the satellite's total mass.

The primordial giant impact disrupted this core, dispersing dense ferromagnetic and noble metal fractions into the upper lithosphere and creating rich metallic ore bodies and mineral nodes. Concurrently, this extensive iron-rich core supports an active geodynamo, generating an intrinsic magnetic field that interacts with Polyphemus's broader magnetosphere.

### Canonical Gaps, Inconsistencies, and Dynamical Tensions

Rigorous examination of primary franchise materials identifies several physical and astrophysical inconsistencies:

1. **Dimensional Discrepancies:** Discrepancies exist between official companion guides. *James Cameron's Avatar: An Activist Survival Guide* assigns Pandora a radius of $0.897\,R_\oplus$ and a mass of $0.72\,M_\oplus$, producing a calculated surface gravity of $0.89\,g$ (though textually rounded to $0.80\,g$). Resolving this tension requires establishing the true planetary heat budget: balanced tidal dissipation can drive vigorous local tectonism without triggering global runaway resurfacing.

## Part B: Planetary Geophysics and Internal Heat Budgets

### The Terrestrial Heat Budget: Primordial and Radiogenic Sources

The thermal evolution of any terrestrial planet or large satellite is governed by the conservation of internal energy:

$$\dot{E}_{\text{internal}} = \dot{E}_{\text{primordial}} + \dot{E}_{\text{radiogenic}} + \dot{E}_{\text{tidal}} - L_{\text{surface}}$$

where $L_{\text{surface}}$ represents the integrated rate of surface heat loss.

* **Primordial Heat:** Derived from accretional gravitational potential energy and core segregation/differentiation.
* **Radiogenic Heat:** Produced by the long-lived decay of $^{238}\text{U}$, $^{235}\text{U}$, $^{232}\text{Th}$, and $^{40}\text{K}$ (with early melting assisted by short-lived $^{26}\text{Al}$).
* **Tidal Heating:** Produced by eccentric orbital forcing ($e > 0$) and viscoelastic mantle friction.
* **Mantle Convection:** Dictates lithospheric transport and powers surface volcanism.

#### Primordial Heat (Accretion and Differentiation)

Primordial energy consists of the thermal budget accumulated during initial accretion and core segregation. The gravitational potential energy released during the accretion of a homogeneous sphere of mass $M$ and radius $R$ is:

$$E_{\text{acc}} = \int_0^M \frac{G m}{r} \, dm = \frac{3}{5}\frac{G M^2}{R}$$

For a Pandora-sized body ($M \approx 4.30 \times 10^{24}\text{ kg}$, $R \approx 5.72 \times 10^6\text{ m}$), this releases $\approx 1.29 \times 10^{32}\text{ J}$.

Subsequent gravitational differentiation—wherein dense molten iron-nickel droplets sink to form the central core—releases additional energy:

$$\Delta E_{\text{diff}} \approx 0.1 \times \frac{G M^2}{R} \approx 2.15 \times 10^{31}\text{ J}$$

While a substantial fraction of this energy escapes during the initial magma ocean stage, secular cooling of the core and mantle continuously delivers a steady heat flux across deep geological time.

#### Radiogenic Heat and Geoneutrino Constraints

Radiogenic heating is driven by the decay of unstable atomic nuclei distributed throughout the silicate mantle and crust. In the contemporary epoch ($t \approx 4.5\text{ Gyr}$), this heating is sustained by four long-lived radionuclides: Uranium-238 ($^{238}\text{U}$), Uranium-235 ($^{235}\text{U}$), Thorium-232 ($^{232}\text{Th}$), and Potassium-40 ($^{40}\text{K}$).

During the earliest stages of planetary accretion ($t < 5\text{ Myr}$), short-lived radionuclides—chiefly Aluminium-26 ($^{26}\text{Al}$, half-life 0.717 Myr) and Iron-60 ($^{60}\text{Fe}$, half-life 2.6 Myr)—dominated the heat budget, rapidly melting planetesimals prior to their incorporation into larger bodies.

For Earth, the total surface heat loss is constrained to $46 \pm 2\text{ TW}$ ($87\text{ mW/m}^2$). Empirical constraints on this heat budget are provided by geoneutrino detections ($\bar{\nu}_e$) from the **KamLAND** and **Borexino** liquid scintillator experiments. These measurements show that radiogenic decay accounts for $20.5^{+8.3}_{-6.2}\text{ TW}$ (roughly 45%) of Earth's total heat loss, with the remainder supplied by primordial secular cooling.

Because radiogenic heat decays exponentially according to $H(t) = \sum C_i q_i e^{-\lambda_i t}$, an ancient terrestrial moon relies on another mechanism to maintain intense geological activity billions of years after formation.

### The Mechanics of Tidal Dissipation: The Primary Engine for Moons

For natural satellites orbiting gas giants, gravitational tidal dissipation can exceed radiogenic and primordial heat by several orders of magnitude.

#### The Physical Mechanism

A satellite orbiting a massive primary experiences a differential gravitational field across its diameter. This tidal gravitational potential deforms the body into a prolate triaxial ellipsoid.

When the orbit possesses a non-zero orbital eccentricity ($e > 0$), the satellite undergoes two distinct cyclic deformations over each orbital cycle:

1. **Radial Variation:** The satellite-primary distance $r(t)$ oscillates between periapsis $a(1-e)$ and apoapsis $a(1+e)$, causing the magnitude of the tidal deformation to scale dynamically as $r^{-3}(t)$.
2. **Librational Variation:** Because the orbital angular velocity changes along the eccentric path while the satellite's rotational speed remains uniform, the tidal bulge oscillates back and forth across the satellite's surface relative to the sub-primary point.

Because planetary interiors behave as **viscoelastic** materials rather than perfectly elastic solids, cyclic mechanical strain lags behind applied gravitational stress by a phase angle $\delta$. This stress-strain hysteresis converts mechanical orbital energy directly into internal thermal energy through anelastic friction within the mantle.

#### Mathematical Formulation

The rate of volumetric tidal energy dissipation $\dot{E}_{\text{tide}}$ for a synchronously rotating satellite in an eccentric orbit is given by the classical Peale-Cassen-Reynolds formulation:

$$\dot{E}_{\text{tide}} = \frac{21}{2} \frac{k_2}{Q} \frac{G M_p^2 R^5 n e^2}{a^6}$$

where $G$ is the gravitational constant, $M_p$ is the mass of the primary planet, $R$ is the mean radius of the satellite, $a$ is the semi-major axis, $e$ is the orbital eccentricity, $n = \sqrt{G M_p / a^3}$ is the orbital mean motion, $k_2$ is the dynamic Love number of degree 2, and $Q$ is the tidal quality factor ($Q^{-1} = \tan \delta$).

Substituting Kepler's third law ($a^6 = G^2 M_p^2 / n^4$) eliminates the semi-major axis and primary mass, yielding a direct dependence on mean motion:

$$\dot{E}_{\text{tide}} = \frac{21}{2} \left(\frac{k_2}{Q}\right) \frac{R^5 n^5 e^2}{G}$$

This formulation demonstrates that tidal heating scales with the fifth power of the satellite's radius ($R^5$), the fifth power of its orbital frequency ($n^5 \propto P^{-5}$), and the square of its eccentricity ($e^2$).

#### The Damping Paradox and Resonant Forcing

Because tidal dissipation converts orbital energy into heat, it circularizes the satellite's orbit over time. The characteristic orbital circularization timescale $\tau_{\text{circ}}$ is:

$$\tau_{\text{circ}} = \frac{2}{21} \frac{Q}{k_2} \frac{M}{M_p} \left(\frac{a}{R}\right)^5 \frac{1}{n}$$

For large satellites orbiting close to gas giants, $\tau_{\text{circ}}$ is typically on the order of $10^6\text{ to }10^8\text{ years}$—a small fraction of planetary lifespans.

Without an external mechanism to continuously pump eccentricity, the orbit rapidly circularizes ($e \to 0$), shutting down tidal dissipation and leaving the interior to freeze into a geologically dormant state.

Therefore, sustained tidal heating across billions of years requires continuous gravitational pumping through **Mean-Motion Resonances (MMR)** with neighboring sibling moons.

### Io as the Archetype: Resonant Forcing and Heat-Pipe Geodynamics

Jupiter's moon Io ($R = 1,821.6\text{ km}$, $M = 8.93 \times 10^{22}\text{ kg}$) is the primary Solar System archetype for tidally driven hyper-volcanism.

Io's orbital eccentricity is maintained at a forced value of $e \approx 0.0041$ by the **Laplace resonance**, a three-body orbital resonance coupling Io, Europa, and Ganymede in a 4:2:1 mean-motion ratio:

$$n_{\text{Io}} - 3n_{\text{Europa}} + 2n_{\text{Ganymede}} = 0$$

Periodic conjunctions between these moons pump their orbital eccentricities, counteracting the circularizing effects of tidal friction.

Data from the Voyager, Galileo, New Horizons, and Juno missions, combined with ground-based infrared radiometry, establish that Io dissipates **100 to 105 TW** of internal heat, producing a global surface heat flux of:

$$F_{\text{Io}} \approx 2.4\text{ to }2.5\text{ W/m}^2$$

This heat flux is nearly 30 times greater than Earth's ($0.087\text{ W/m}^2$).

#### Heat-Pipe Volcanism

Rather than dissipating heat through mobile-lid plate tectonics, Io operates in a **heat-pipe mode** (Moore and Webb, 2013):

1. Intense tidal dissipation within Io's partially molten asthenosphere generates large volumes of ultramafic magma.


2. Magma ascends through the lithosphere via narrow volcanic conduits, erupting directly onto the surface at temperatures exceeding 1300 to 1600 K.


3. Continuous volcanic resurfacing deposits fresh lava flows over older strata, burying cold crustal material and advecting it downward toward the mantle.


4. This downward advection keeps the lithosphere cold, thick ($\sim 30\text{ to }50\text{ km}$), and mechanically rigid, suppressing plate tectonics while supporting high volcanic throughput.

### Internal Heat Transport and Magmagenesis

To drive surface volcanism, thermal energy generated deep within the mantle must be transported to the base of the lithosphere.

#### Conduction versus Solid-State Convection

Thermal conduction is described by Fourier's Law:

$$\mathbf{q} = -k \nabla T$$

Silicate rocks possess low thermal conductivities ($k \approx 2\text{--}4\text{ W}\cdot\text{m}^{-1}\text{K}^{-1}$), making pure conductive transport inefficient across thick planetary layers.

When thermal buoyancy overcomes viscous resistance, heat transport transitions to solid-state mantle convection. This convective vigor is governed by the dimensionless **Rayleigh Number ($Ra$)**:

$$Ra = \frac{\rho g \alpha \Delta T D^3}{\kappa \eta}$$

where $\rho$ is mantle density, $g$ is local gravitational acceleration, $\alpha$ is the thermal expansion coefficient ($\sim 2 \times 10^{-5}\text{ K}^{-1}$), $\Delta T$ is the superadiabatic temperature contrast, $D$ is mantle thickness, $\kappa = k/(\rho c_p)$ is thermal diffusivity ($\sim 10^{-6}\text{ m}^2/\text{s}$), and $\eta$ is dynamic viscosity ($\sim 10^{19}\text{ to }10^{22}\text{ Pa}\cdot\text{s}$).

When $Ra > Ra_{\text{crit}} \approx 10^3$, convection begins. Planetary mantles typically operate at $Ra \sim 10^6\text{ to }10^8$, driving solid-state mantle flow at rates of centimeters to decimeters per year.

#### Partial Melting and Melt Extraction

As convective mantle plumes ascend adiabatically, decompression lowers the solidus temperature below the local mantle adiabat:

$$\left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial P}\right)_{\text{solidus}} > \left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial P}\right)_{\text{adiabat}} = \frac{\alpha T}{\rho c_p}$$

This decompression triggers **partial melting** of peridotite mantle into basaltic liquid. Because liquid magma is less dense than the surrounding solid rock matrix ($\Delta \rho \approx 300\text{ to }500\text{ kg/m}^3$), positive buoyancy forces drive it upward through interstitial pores via porous Darcy flow, concentrating into dyke networks and magma chambers that supply surface volcanoes.

### Electromagnetic Thermodynamics: Why Magnetic Fields Do Not Melt Mantles

A recurring misconception in science fiction lore is that planetary magnetic fields act as primary thermal engines that melt rock into magma. Thermodynamic analysis demonstrates why this is physically impossible.

#### The Work-Energy Theorem for Magnetic Fields

The force $\mathbf{F}$ acting on a charged particle $q$ moving with velocity $\mathbf{v}$ in an electromagnetic field is described by the Lorentz force:

$$\mathbf{F} = q(\mathbf{E} + \mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B})$$

The rate of mechanical work done on the particle by the magnetic field component is:

$$\frac{dW_{\text{mag}}}{dt} = \mathbf{F}_{\text{mag}} \cdot \mathbf{v} = q(\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B}) \cdot \mathbf{v} \equiv 0$$

Because the magnetic force vector $\mathbf{F}_{\text{mag}} = q(\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B})$ is always orthogonal to the instantaneous velocity vector $\mathbf{v}$, **a static magnetic field does zero net work on moving charges.** It alters particle trajectories, but it cannot continuously supply kinetic or thermal energy to a system.

#### Electromagnetic Induction Heating Constraints

Thermal dissipation from a magnetic field occurs only when the magnetic flux through a conductor varies in time ($\partial \mathbf{B}/\partial t \neq 0$), as described by Faraday’s law of induction:

$$\nabla \times \mathbf{E} = -\frac{\partial \mathbf{B}}{\partial t}$$

This electric field induces eddy current densities $\mathbf{J} = \sigma \mathbf{E}$ (where $\sigma$ is electrical conductivity), generating Joule heating:

$$P_{\text{Joule}} = \mathbf{J} \cdot \mathbf{E} = \frac{\mathbf{J}^2}{\sigma} = \sigma |\mathbf{E}|^2$$

Analytical formulations developed by Chyba, Hand, and Thomas (2021) demonstrate that while time-varying magnetic induction can generate measurable induction fields (such as those used by the Galileo mission to confirm subsurface salty oceans on Europa and Callisto), the resulting volumetric heating is negligible compared to tidal dissipation.

For a large planetary satellite with an electrical conductivity $\sigma \approx 10^{-2}\text{ to }10^{-4}\text{ S/m}$ (typical of warm silicate rock) orbiting within a Jovian magnetosphere ($\Delta B \approx 10^2\text{ to }10^3\text{ nT}$ variations), the total global induction heating power is:

$$\dot{E}_{\text{induction}} \sim 10^7\text{ to }10^9\text{ W} \quad (\mathbf{0.00001\text{ to }0.001\text{ GW}})$$

Compared to tidal dissipation ($10^{14}\text{ W} = 100\text{ TW}$) or radiogenic heating ($10^{13}\text{ W} = 10\text{ TW}$), induction heating is smaller by **five to seven orders of magnitude**.

Furthermore, superconductors exhibit the **Meissner-Ochsenfeld effect**, expelling magnetic fields from their interior ($B = 0$). Internal induction currents are confined to a thin London penetration depth ($\lambda_L \sim 10\text{--}100\text{ nm}$), which further suppresses volumetric induction heating throughout the moon's bulk interior.

#### Dynamo Causality

A planetary magnetic field is an *energy sink*, not an energy source. Planetary dynamos operate by converting convective kinetic energy into magnetic field energy within an electrically conductive liquid metal outer core:

$$\frac{\partial \mathbf{B}}{\partial t} = \nabla \times (\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{B}) + \eta_m \nabla^2 \mathbf{B}$$

The convective fluid velocity $\mathbf{u}$ is driven by internal thermal buoyancy (primordial heat and core cooling) and compositional buoyancy (light-element exclusion during inner-core crystallization). The magnetic field is a *consequence* of core cooling; it does not generate the heat that powers the planet.

#### Devastation Mechanisms

The total destruction of the Mangkwan ancestral Hometree (*Kelutrel*) and the regional biome requires a **VEI 6 to VEI 7 caldera-forming event**, governed by three primary physical processes:

1. **Pyroclastic Density Currents (PDCs):** High-velocity, ground-hugging mixtures of superheated gas (500 to 1000 K), pumice, and ash moving down volcanic slopes at speeds of 50 to $> 150\text{ m/s}$. PDCs incinerate biological structures and sever the electrochemical neural root connections of the Pandoran biome.
2. **Heavy Tephra and Ash Fallout:** Plinian eruptive plumes inject tens of cubic kilometers of fine silicate and vitric ash into the stratosphere. Regional ash fallout blankets the landscape under meters of dense tephra, suffocating flora, collapsing structures, and transforming soil into sterile regolith.
3. **Volcanic Smog and Atmospheric Acidification:** Degassing of sulfur dioxide ($\text{SO}_2$), hydrogen sulfide ($\text{H}_2\text{S}$), and hydrogen fluoride ($\text{HF}$) produces persistent volcanic smog (VOG) and acid rain, corroding foliage and disrupting the local ecosystem for decades.

### Quantitative Synthesis and Order-of-Magnitude Modeling for Pandora

To determine the orbital conditions that allow Pandora to support a volcanically active surface capable of producing the Ashlands event, the tidal dissipation equation can be evaluated across reasonable parameter ranges for the Polyphemus-Pandora system.

#### The Pandoran Geophysical Optimum

This quantitative analysis yields three key insights into Pandora's planetary geophysics:

1. An orbital eccentricity of $e \approx 0.0010$ combined with a standard mantle rheology ($k_2/Q \approx 10^{-3}$) generates **$\approx 330\text{ TW}$** of continuous tidal power, producing an average surface heat flux of **$\approx 0.80\text{ W/m}^2$**. This surface flux is approximately **nine times Earth's total heat flow ($0.087\text{ W/m}^2$)**, but comfortably below the threshold for catastrophic biospheric sterilization or runaway ocean vaporization.


2. This thermal energy budget of $0.80\text{ W/m}^2$ drives rapid mantle convection, fast plate recycling, high magma generation rates, and frequent large-scale volcanic eruptions (such as the VEI 6–7 event that created the Ashlands) without stripping the atmosphere or disrupting the global climate.


3. To maintain this non-zero eccentricity ($e \sim 0.001$) over the multi-billion-year age of the Alpha Centauri system, Pandora must be locked in a **mean-motion resonance** (e.g., a 2:1 or 3:2 resonance) with one or more of Polyphemus’s 13 other satellites. Gravitational interactions with these sibling moons pump Pandora’s eccentricity, counteracting tidal circularization.

## Part C: Comparative Planetology and Analogical Synthesis

To bridge the Earth-science literature and the world of Pandora, the exomoon can be understood as a larger, water-rich analogue of Jupiter's moon Io. Both bodies are situated within intense gravitational wells and rely on orbital resonance to power their internal geodynamics.

* **The Resonant Engine:** Io maintains hyper-volcanism via the Laplace resonance with Europa and Ganymede. Pandora relies on an analogous multi-moon resonance with Polyphemus's 13 other satellites to maintain an orbital eccentricity of $e \approx 0.001$ over billions of years.


* **Thermal Dissipation:** Pandora's viscoelastic mantle dissipates roughly **330 TW** of internal heat—over three times Io's total heat output (105 TW) and seven times Earth's (46 TW). Because Pandora's surface area is twelve times larger than Io's, this energy is distributed as a surface heat flux of $\approx 0.80\text{ W/m}^2$, allowing surface oceans and complex life to thrive alongside active volcanism.


* **The Limits of Magnetism:** While room-temperature superconducting unobtanium creates macroscopic magnetic levitation (supporting the Hallelujah Mountains), electromagnetic fields cannot perform net work on silicate mantles. Magma generation requires mechanical energy conversion via tidal dissipation.

## Part D: Synthesis and Geodynamic Conclusions

Pandora remains volcanically active billions of years after formation through fundamental planetary mechanics: **resonant tidal dissipation**.

While superconducting unobtanium and flux vortices produce macroscopic magnetic levitation (such as the Hallelujah Mountains), electromagnetic fields cannot perform continuous net mechanical work on neutral silicate mantles, and induction heating is orders of magnitude too weak to melt rock.

Instead, Pandora’s geological activity is sustained by an orbital architecture analogous to Jupiter's Galilean moons:

* Pandora is continuously flexed by Polyphemus's gravitational field, dissipating roughly **330 TW** of heat through its viscoelastic mantle.


* Orbital eccentricity is maintained across deep time by mean-motion resonances with sibling moons.


* This tidal energy drives vigorous mantle convection and rapid plate recycling, generating frequent volcanic activity.


* Under these geodynamic conditions, regional caldera collapses and high-intensity Plinian eruptions are inevitable, providing a sound physical foundation for the catastrophe that destroyed the Mangkwan ancestral Hometree and created the Ashlands.
